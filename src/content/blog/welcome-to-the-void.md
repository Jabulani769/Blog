The darkeness, the horror, the blank and unredable, willing to take in everyone who dares to step close and those who submit their mess (their chaotic code that mirrors ur own experience). It feels like almost nothing is happening inside you, it calms your soul when it executes. At the same time its joy wrapped in frustration, a loop that pulls and pushes when ever you need. AI speaks in riddles or maybe thats what i have seen, it mostly offers guidance, solutions then vanishes. Like the times you find yourself vibe coding n start to feel the euphoric excitement of the code. Later to find an error that knocks you and the model together.

The void holds more than we imagine. Majority dont understand, when u deside to roll with the void _"What breaks first"_ should be the question that roams through your soul, it may be the code or the mind. Ironically, we enter the void with a broken system or hunger for information only to come out with perfect systems n rotten brains claiming the victory. <span class="font-semibold"><mark class="bg-amber-100">_"blessed are those that walk into the horrors of the void and come out unskinned"_ </mark></span>. Being a space where no one judges, the particles follow your dance. u become obsessed and thats the disadvanteage of staying too long in the void.

The thoughts of building a personal space on the web (chasing big projects) and dumping research, sacrificing [cognitive functions in the name of the future](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2025/11/is-ai-dulling-our-minds/) has been the inevitable cost of knowledge dissemination in the digital age. Yet the void is not endless despair. Small sparks appear in the chaos, there is no hero here. The void becomes a garden where fragments are curated n chaos breeds insight.

**Step in. Step out.**  
Or [remain n play it safe](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/careers/future-of-work/article-tk-to-avoid-ai-rotting-your-brain-use-these-tips-for-exercise/) ... its all on you.
