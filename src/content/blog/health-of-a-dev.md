Catching the green dot is important to most developers, we most of the times forget about ourselves. We spend counteless hours (countless, really?) chasing useful and useless solutions, fixing bugs, keeping the mighty streak alive. <i>"Just one more commit"</i>, while this can make you feel dedicated but it costs us something far more important: OUR HEALTH.

## Physical fitness and Mental health
As it sounds these are different terms, one is about the condition of your body (physical) and the other is avout your mind. Developer wise these two influence each other. A neglected body leads to a tired body, unfocused mind which ends up you writing some gurbage code.
